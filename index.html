what jio carries between conversations. it writes these itself as things come up, and it is the reason a turn doesn't have to resend your whole history to know who it's talking to — the transcript stays in the chat, the gist lives here. everything on this page is yours to change: edit a line, delete one, or add your own.

    key pool

    jio runs on free keys donated by people who use it — groq, nvidia, gemini and cohere. keys are picked least-used-first; one that hits its limit rests exactly as long as the provider asks, a revoked one is dropped, and a request rolls on to the next key or the next provider rather than failing. keys stay on the server — no browser ever sees them.

    donate a key

    your key is stored server-side and never sent to another browser. jio reaches for the least-used healthy key first.
